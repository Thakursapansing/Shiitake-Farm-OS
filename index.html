Updated Block Diagnosis SOP

Green Mold (Trichoderma) — 10% Lime Solution Protocol

When green mold patches appear during late incubation, browning, or between flushes on poplar sawdust/chip blocks, do not use Hydrogen Peroxide. Follow the farm's alkaline suppression treatment:

  • Preparation: Mix Hydrated Lime in clean water at a 10% rate (100g Lime per 1 Liter of water).
  • Application: Gently wipe surface spores with a cloth dipped in lime water, then apply or spray the 10% lime solution in water directly onto the affected spot.
  • Action Mechanism: Raises surface pH above 9.5, halting Trichoderma growth while preserving browned Shiitake mycelium.
Flush 2 Triggering SOP

Flush 2 Water Spray & Block Weighing Procedure

  1. Resting Period (14–21 Days): Rest blocks on racks after Flush 1 harvest.
  2. Primary Spray Trigger (4–5 Days): After resting 14–21 days, start spraying water 5 to 6 times a day continuously for 4 to 5 days until Flush 2 pins appear.
  3. Fallback Weigh & Re-Soak Check: If Flush 2 does not appear after the 4–5 day spray cycle, weigh the block before re-soaking for 12 to 24 hours in cold water to trigger Flush 2.
